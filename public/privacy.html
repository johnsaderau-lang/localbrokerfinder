Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

The Finance Firm Pty Ltd (ABN 31 670 677 513) ("we", "us", "our") operates localbrokerfinder.com.au. We are committed to handling your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

What we collect

When you use our broker matching service and submit your details, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Your suburb or postcode
  • Information about what you are looking to do (buy, refinance, invest)
  • Information about your borrowing situation (loan amount, employment, deposit, where provided)
  • Technical information automatically (IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited)

Why we collect it

  • To deliver the broker matching service you have requested
  • To contact you about your enquiry, including by phone, SMS, or email
  • To match you with a licensed mortgage broker based on your location and needs
  • To improve our website and service
  • To comply with our legal and regulatory obligations

Who we share it with

We may share your personal information with:

  • Mortgage brokers within The Finance Firm and our partner network, for the purpose of providing the service you requested
  • Australian Finance Group Ltd (AFG) and other aggregators or lenders, where required to provide credit assistance
  • Service providers who help us operate our website and CRM (e.g. data hosting, email delivery, customer relationship platforms)
  • Government or regulatory bodies where required by law

We do not sell your personal information.

How we store and protect it

Your information is stored on secure cloud-based platforms based in Australia or with reputable international providers. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, unauthorised access, and disclosure.

Direct marketing

By submitting your details, you consent to us contacting you about your enquiry and about other home-loan related products and services we believe may be of interest. You can opt out at any time by replying "STOP" to any SMS, clicking "unsubscribe" in any email, or contacting us directly.

Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use our website, run advertising campaigns (including via Meta and Google), and improve user experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect website functionality.

Accessing or correcting your information

You can request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us at hi@thefinancefirm.com.au. We will respond within 30 days.

Complaints

If you have a privacy complaint, please contact us first at hi@thefinancefirm.com.au. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can refer the matter to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA) on 1800 931 678 or via afca.org.au, or to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) on 1300 363 992 or via oaic.gov.au.
